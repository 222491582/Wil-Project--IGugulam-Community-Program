Business Model Canvas

iGugulam Community Program — After-School Homework Support, Gugulethu

Why this model: a 27-parent survey found 96% have a school-going child, over 80% only get home between 3–7pm, 37% of children spend that time with family/relatives and 33% are home alone — while 70% of parents said they'd be "very interested" in an after-school programme like this in Gugulethu.
